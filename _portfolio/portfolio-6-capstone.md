Capstone project (Fall 2024) and bachelor's thesis. Advisor: Prof. <b>Taejin Ahn</b>. Developed a <b>language-model-based RNA-seq ETL workflow</b> for ovarian cancer classification. RNA-seq data from patients and healthy individuals were embedded using <b>ProtBERT</b> and classified with a <b>Bi-LSTM</b>, achieving a test <b>AUC of 0.71</b>. <a href='/files/2024-11-28-capstone.pdf'>[PPT]</a> <a href='/files/language.pdf'>[Report]</a>
