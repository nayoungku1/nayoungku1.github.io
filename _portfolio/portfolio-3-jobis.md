A team project from the 2025 Summer Big Data Camp (organized by the Big Data Program at Handong Global University for students of COSS-affiliated universities), building an AI agent for <b>personalized interview preparation</b>. Existing AI interview tools struggle to connect job requirements with a candidate's background, rely on generic questions that miss current job trends, and give little objective, actionable feedback. JOBIS generates <b>job-specific questions</b> from a user's resume, the job posting, and web data. My role: developing the <b>personal context summarization model</b>, managing the GitHub repository, and preparing the presentation materials. <a href='https://github.com/nayoungku1/JOBis'>[GitHub]</a>
